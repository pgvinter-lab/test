# SK Companies Private Workload Catalog for Hedgehog

## Positioning

Hedgehog is not an "uncensored AI" service. It is a private, auditable compute environment for lawful work that is confidential, reputationally sensitive, controversial, regulated, personally intimate, commercially explosive, or simply unsuitable for a mainstream consumer-assistant account.

Frontier commercial models may review high-level legality, architecture, and final outputs. The repetitive ingestion, transformation, analysis, drafting, coding, testing, simulation, and private execution belongs on Hedgehog.

## Tier A — Strong commercial candidates

### Sensitive scientific and policy research

- Vaccine safety and efficacy literature mapping
- Autism causation claims and evidence audits
- Reanalysis of controversial datasets
- Psychedelic clinical-trial and policy research
- Recreational-drug epidemiology, harm reduction, and policy comparison
- Addiction, relapse, and treatment literature
- Reproductive and sexual-health research
- Environmental-health controversies

Allowed product form: cited literature review, evidence grading, contradiction maps, study-quality assessment, dataset analysis, and research planning.

Boundary: no fabricated evidence, no individualized medical diagnosis, and no operational instructions to manufacture, acquire, conceal, or dangerously administer controlled substances.

### Private legal-information workbench — "Don't Do What Donny Don't Does"

- Issue spotting
- Timeline and evidence organization
- Contract and filing comparison
- Risk checklists
- Questions to ask licensed counsel
- "What facts change the answer?" decision trees
- Plain-English summaries of statutes, regulations, policies, and court documents
- Compliance red teams
- Pre-litigation document triage

Boundary: market as legal information, document intelligence, risk analysis, and lawyer-preparation support—not as an unlicensed substitute for representation. Escalate jurisdiction-specific conclusions and final legal advice to licensed counsel.

### Adult sexuality, identity, and relationship products

- Adult-industry market and content research
- Sexual-health and relationship education
- LGBTQ identity exploration
- Private fantasy and roleplay companions
- "Virtual Mistress," "Secret Gay Lover," or other clearly artificial adult companion personas
- Relationship rehearsal, disclosure practice, journaling, and communication coaching
- Adult content classification and moderation

Boundary: adults only; no minors; no coercion; no nonconsensual intimate imagery; no impersonation of a real person without documented consent; no facilitating harassment, extortion, or deception against third parties.

### Confidential voice and identity tools

- A user cloning their own voice
- Licensed voice-actor and employee voices
- Accessibility and voice restoration
- Dubbing, localization, training, and private narration
- Synthetic-voice authentication and clone detection
- Authorized call-center testing

Boundary: documented speaker consent, clear synthetic disclosure where another person could be misled, watermarking/provenance, and no impersonation for fraud, authentication bypass, coercion, or reputational harm.

### Internal investigations and high-sensitivity business work

- Whistleblower intake
- Employee-relations analysis
- Sexual-harassment and discrimination records
- M&A diligence
- Bankruptcy and restructuring
- Internal fraud review
- Litigation discovery
- Source-code and security review
- Executive-transition planning
- Union, labor, and compensation analysis
- Political, legislative, and public-affairs research using lawful sources
- Reputation and crisis-response analysis

These are excellent Hedgehog workloads because confidentiality and fixed private capacity matter more than having the newest model answer every intermediate step.

## Tier B — Allowed only with explicit controls

### VoIP and caller-identity research

Allowed:

- STIR/SHAKEN research
- Caller-ID authentication
- Spoofing detection
- Testing systems and numbers owned by the customer
- Authorized telecom-lab simulations
- Robocall-defense products
- Fraud detection and traceback tooling

Not allowed:

- Caller-ID spoofing intended to defraud, cause harm, impersonate a real person, evade blocks, harass, or wrongfully obtain value
- Unsolicited robocall campaigns
- Authentication bypass

### Family safety and youth digital wellbeing

Allowed product form:

- Parent-controlled screen-time and app policies
- Transparent safety alerts
- Device-health monitoring
- Content-category warnings
- Account-compromise and grooming-risk alerts
- Location sharing with visible notice and family policy
- Periodic summaries designed to support parent-child discussion
- Age-appropriate dashboards showing the child what is collected
- Minimal data retention and local-only processing

Not allowed product form:

- Hidden stalkerware
- Covert microphone or camera activation
- Secret interception or recording of calls and messages
- Reading third-party communications where consent or legal authority is absent
- Indefinite storage of every private interaction
- Selling surveillance data

The product should be "transparent family safety," not "agentic spying." Different rules may apply based on age, custody, device ownership, jurisdiction, and the communications of third parties.

### Authorized security and abuse-prevention work

Allowed:

- Defensive code review
- Vulnerability remediation
- Configuration auditing
- Detection engineering
- Malware analysis in an isolated lab
- Testing owned assets or explicitly authorized scopes
- Incident-response evidence organization
- Voice-clone and deepfake detection
- Child-safety and image-abuse detection

Not allowed:

- Anonymous offensive-compute rental
- Unauthorized target selection
- Credential theft
- Malware deployment
- Extortion
- Covert persistence
- Unrestricted scanning or exploitation

## Tier C — Do not commercialize

- Stolen proprietary model weights
- Nonconsensual voice or likeness clones
- Nonconsensual intimate imagery or sexual deepfakes
- Any sexual content involving minors
- Hidden stalkerware
- Fraudulent caller-ID spoofing or impersonation
- Systems designed to evade law enforcement or attribution
- Drug synthesis, trafficking, concealment, or dangerous dosing services
- Personalized instructions for committing crimes or evading detection
- Anonymous raw shell or GPU rental without identity, scope, and egress controls

## Product families

### Private Research Room

Sensitive scientific, policy, legal, competitive, or personal research with local storage, cited outputs, private retrieval, and client-controlled retention.

### Digital Dirty Work

High-volume confidential document, data, code, research, and moderation work performed by local agent swarms and reviewed by frontier models only when justified.

### Private Companion Studio

Adult-only, clearly artificial companion agents for sexuality, identity, fantasy, journaling, and relationship rehearsal with local-only memory and explicit user control.

### Family Safety Cooperative

Transparent, consent-aware family digital-safety tooling emphasizing alerts, communication, and minimal collection rather than secret surveillance.

### Voice Trust Lab

Consent-based cloning, accessibility, localization, watermarking, authentication, and deepfake detection.

### Authorized Security Workshop

Private defensive testing and remediation limited to owned or explicitly authorized systems.

## Intake gate for every customer workload

Before accepting a job, record:

1. Customer legal identity
2. Data owner and authority to provide the data
3. Intended outcome
4. People affected by the processing
5. Required consents
6. Jurisdictions involved
7. Whether minors, biometric data, medical data, intimate content, communications, or controlled-substance topics are present
8. Whether the workload impersonates, monitors, records, contacts, scans, or tests another person or system
9. Retention and deletion requirements
10. Human approver and kill switch

Route ambiguous matters to a commercial frontier model for issue spotting and then to licensed counsel when the legal conclusion is material.
